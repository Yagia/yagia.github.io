1. Who we are and the short version

The app is made by Yağız Akdeniz, an individual developer in Türkiye (“we”). Contact: yagizakdeniz0@gmail.com.

You do not need an account. We do not sell your data. We have no server of our own: what leaves your phone goes to the services named below, only to make a feature work, to show ads, or to count use and crashes. Everything else – your settings, saved places, notifications and alarms – stays on your device.

Some features are not yet in the Android app: the shared khatm (Ortak Hatim), the “Buy me a coffee” purchase and the Quran. This page already describes them, so that it stays true when they arrive; each section says where a feature exists today.

2. Location

Location is optional: you can always pick a place by hand, and you can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's settings. If you allow it, the app uses your position to choose the nearest place and its prayer-time method.

  • Prayer times. For places in Türkiye and others served by Türkiye's Presidency of Religious Affairs (Diyanet), the app sends only the place's number – not your position – to Diyanet (awqatsalah.diyanet.gov.tr, namazvakitleri.diyanet.gov.tr) or to the mirrors that republish Diyanet's tables (ezanvakti.emushaf.net, ezanvakti.imsakiyem.com). For calculated timetables it sends the coordinates of your position (approximate or precise, as you allowed) or of the chosen place to Aladhan (api.aladhan.com), which answers with the month's times. 25 countries' timetables are calculated on the phone and send nothing.
  • The place's name. The phone turns your position into a place name with the system's service: Google Play services on Android, Apple's geocoder on iPhone.
  • Nearby mosques. Your position is sent to OpenStreetMap's Overpass servers (overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems, overpass.openstreetmap.fr) to find mosques around you. On Android the map's tiles come from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), which receives the map area shown and your internet address.
  • When. On Android only while you use the app (no background location). On iPhone, if you also allow “Always”, the app can follow your position while closed so that times and notifications match where you are.
  • Ads (section 3) may estimate an approximate location from your internet address.

We keep no copy of your position; the last one is stored only on your device. The services above receive it to answer the request; their own policies apply to their servers.

3. Device and other identifiers (ads, statistics, Ortak Hatim)

  • Ads: the app shows banner and app-open ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use the device's advertising ID (on iPhone only if you allow tracking), your internet address, ad views and taps and diagnostic data to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud; Google uses this data under its own policy. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app first shows Google's consent message; your choice is stored on the device and can be changed under Settings → Privacy Options. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Statistics and crash reports: Google Firebase gives the app an installation identifier (sections 4 and 5).
  • Ortak Hatim (iPhone today, Android later): Firebase Anonymous Authentication gives the app a random user identifier with no name, email or password (sections 6 and 7).

4. How the app is used (usage statistics)

Google Firebase Analytics records which screens are opened and similar events, with the device model, system version, language and country. We use it to see which features are used, never to identify you. Where consent is required (section 3), statistics are collected only after the consent step and according to your choice. Ads record ad views and taps (section 3).

5. Crash reports and diagnostics

When the app crashes, Google Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report (where in the app it happened, device model, system version, free memory and similar). We use it only to fix errors.

6. Your nickname in Ortak Hatim

iPhone today, Android later. Ortak Hatim is optional. When you start or join a shared khatm, the nickname you type is stored in Google Cloud Firestore and shown to everyone who has the group's invite code or link, next to the parts (juz) you took.

7. Group titles, juz claims and reports in Ortak Hatim

iPhone today, Android later. Firestore also stores the group's title, which juz each member took and when they marked it read, the organizer's anonymous identifier, and the members the organizer removed. If you report a person or a group, the report (the reason, the group, the reported member and your anonymous identifier) is stored where only we can read it, so that we can act on it.

8. Purchases

iPhone today, Android later. The optional “Buy me a coffee” is a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play; Apple or Google handles the payment and we never see payment details. The app receives the purchase record (which product and when) to remove the ads for a month; on iPhone through RevenueCat, a purchase service, under a random app user identifier.

9. Backup and sync

  • Android: the system's backup can copy the app's settings, saved places, prayer-tracker logs and language to your own Google account, encrypted by the system. We cannot read it. You can turn it off in the phone's settings.
  • iPhone: if the iCloud switch in Settings is on, bookmarks, notes, reading statistics, the khatm plan, trackers, your own dhikrs and favourites are synced through your own iCloud account. We cannot read it.

10. Content the app downloads

The app downloads prayer times, the cards of the day and – where the feature exists – Quran texts, translations, tafsir, recitations and guides from public services such as Diyanet, Aladhan, alquran.cloud, quran.com, QuranEnc, HadeethEnc, reminder.dev and dua-dhikr. These requests carry the item and the language asked for, nothing about you beyond what every internet request carries (your internet address and the device type). Links to other websites open those sites; their policies apply there.

11. Keeping and deleting data, your rights

  • We keep Ortak Hatim data until it is deleted: give back your juz or leave a group in the app; an organizer can remove a member or close the group. Statistics and crash reports are kept as long as Google's retention settings for the project allow.
  • Write to yagizakdeniz0@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete all of it (your Ortak Hatim nickname, claims and anonymous identifier included). Under the law that applies to you – for example the EU's GDPR or Türkiye's personal data protection law (KVKK, No. 6698) – you may also object to processing or complain to your data protection authority.
  • Data may be processed on servers outside your country (Google, Apple, the services in sections 2 and 10).
  • Requests to the services named on this page use HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.
  • We may disclose data if the law requires it.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. The section for learning the Arabic letters and short surahs shows no ads and offers no purchases. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

13. Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the new text is published on this page with a new date; important changes are announced beforehand on this page and in the app's release notes (“What's new”). Questions: yagizakdeniz0@gmail.com.
